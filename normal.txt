The crimson sun dipped below the jagged horizon, casting long, skeletal shadows across the dunes.
Elara adjusted her respirator, the rhythmic hiss masking the howling wind.
She had been tracking the anomalous signal for three grueling days.
Her scanner beeped, a sharp sound that pierced the desolate silence.
Just ahead, half-buried in the rust-colored sand, a metallic structure gleamed.
It wasn't the wreckage of a standard cargo skiff.
The metal was dark, pulsing with a faint, iridescent violet light.
She slid down the dune, boots sinking deep into the abrasive dust.
As she approached, a low hum vibrated through the soles of her boots.
The structure was a perfect obelisk, untouched by the planet's corrosive atmosphere.
She reached out a gloved hand, hesitating an inch from the surface.
The moment her fingertips made contact, the humming escalated into a deafening roar.
Sand blasted away from the base, revealing a series of intricate, glowing glyphs.
Elara didn't recognize the language, though it felt strangely familiar in her mind.
A seam appeared in the flawless metal, sliding open with a hiss of escaping pressure.
Frigid air poured from the opening, smelling of ozone and ancient storms.
She unholstered her plasma pistol, stepping cautiously into the darkened threshold.
Inside, the walls were lined with crystalline conduits, pulsing in sync with her heartbeat.
At the center of the chamber rested a pedestal made of shattered starlight.
Hovering above it was a sphere of pure, contained energy.
"The Oracle," she whispered, her breath catching in her throat.
Legends claimed it held the memories of the universe before the Great Fracture.
Suddenly, the entrance sealed shut behind her, plunging the room into twilight.
The sphere drifted downward, moving autonomously toward her face.
She raised her arms defensively, but the energy passed right through her armor.
A flood of visions tore through her consciousness: dying stars, rising empires, and a dark entity devouring galaxies.
When she finally opened her eyes, she was no longer in the obelisk.
She was standing on the bridge of a colossal starship, viewing a vibrant, unscarred Earth.
A voice echoed in her mind, clear and commanding.
"You are the final vessel, Elara; it is time to rewrite the end."
